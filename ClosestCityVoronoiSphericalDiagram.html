Closest-city map

Pick 4 cities. Every point on Earth is coloured by whichever one is nearest (great-circle distance).

Runs offline from a built-in list of ~200 major world cities (no live geocoding) — type a name and pick it from the suggestions. Projection is equirectangular; distances are true great-circle (haversine), so the coloured regions curve the way they do on a globe.
